The quest for 99.999% uptime is challenging but achievable.

Five nines reliability means your system is available 99.999% of the time. That’s only 5.26 minutes of downtime per yearCalculated as: 365.25 days × 24 hours × 60 minutes × 0.00001 = 5.26 minutes .

Key Strategies

  1. Redundancy at every layer
  2. Automated failover
  3. Comprehensive monitoring
  4. Regular disaster recovery drills


High availability architecture diagram

The path to five nines requires both technical excellence and operational discipline.
