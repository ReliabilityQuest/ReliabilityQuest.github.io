{% newthought 'You know what you need?'%} Honesty. Your business is humming along with its healthy operating margin, but no one gives a shit. Your improved IRR doesn't mean a thing. You know it was a mix of luck and mediocre decision making. If it hadn't been good fortune, would it have neen your call? Annie Duke calls this *resulting*, but it's you’re ego speaking.

Want to be honest? cut the bullshit. Burn the success theater to the ground. Where you're going you don't need false pretense. There's a core of what's real. You know how your business works. But that doesn't mean you know everything about automation/data/software. A cobbled together report and some good operational numbers does not a digital transformation make.

There's a better way.
